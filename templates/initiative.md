## Executive Summary

2–3 sentences: why this matters, what we're going to do, and the expected impact,
expressed in terms of the initiative's final score and confidence.

The factual problem description, its severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low), and
references to the evidence that substantiates it.

## Evidence

## Root Cause

RootCauseAnalysis is available, this section is explicitly marked `[UNKNOWN: ...]`
rather than guessed.
